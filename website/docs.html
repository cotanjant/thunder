How to use Thunder

Everything happens inside the app on your disk. No account, no cloud sync and no internet connection required.

Transport and dockable workspace

The top transport bar controls playback, tempo, swing and recording. Panels dock and rearrange to fit your screen.

Playback and timing

Play starts, Stop halts and Reset jumps back to bar 1. Switch between Pat to loop one pattern and Song to play the full playlist. Set BPM directly or click Tap in time with music, add Swing for a loose groove, and turn on Click for the metronome.

Dockable panels

Drag panel tabs to dock them side by side or stack them. Use F5 through F9 to jump to the Playlist, Channel Rack, Piano Roll, Browser and Mixer, and save or reset your workspace layout at any time.

Channel rack, synths and CLAP plugins

Add as many channels as your song needs, reorder them, set choke groups and route each one to a mixer insert.

Steps and Drum Rack

Click steps in the channel rack to place beats, or open the 16-pad Drum Rack to layer one-shots with individual pitch, pan and choke groups. M mutes a channel and S solos it.

SuperSynth and FM synth

SuperSynth gives you three oscillators, unison detune, a multimode filter and an LFO matrix. The 4-operator FM synth covers electric pianos, metallic percussion and deep basses.

Stereo sampler

Load WAV, MP3, OGG or FLAC files from the browser by dragging them onto a channel. Adjust start and end points, loop regions, reverse playback and granular time-stretch.

CLAP plugins

Scan your system plugin folders to load third-party CLAP instruments and insert effects. Plugin parameters, native windows and preset states save right inside your project file.

Melodies in the piano roll

Click a channel name to open its notes in the piano roll. Keys outside your selected scale shade darker so melodies stay in key.

Drawing and editing

  • Click empty grid space to place a note, drag to move it, or pull its right edge to resize.
  • Use marquee select, paint and slice tools to edit whole phrases at once.
  • Enable Slide on a note for smooth pitch glides into the next note.
  • Ghost notes show other channels in the same pattern behind your active melody.

Helpers and lanes

  • Snap notes to the grid, quantize timing, humanize velocity or strum chords.
  • Stamp major, minor and seventh chords or generate arpeggios from held notes.
  • Drag velocity bars underneath or draw filter and volume curves in the property lane.

Playlist, recording and sample editor

Arrange patterns, audio recordings and automation curves across multi-track lanes.

Multi-track arrangement

Place Pattern, Audio and Automation clips anywhere on the timeline. Set loop and punch-in/out regions, and bend automation curves with tension handles.

Hardware MIDI and audio recording

Play instruments from a USB MIDI controller or your computer keyboard, and map hardware knobs with MIDI learn. Arm an audio input to monitor and record live takes directly to disk, then trim, fade, normalize or slice transients in the built-in sample editor.

Mixer, effects and export

Route channels to mixer inserts with automatic delay compensation and LUFS/RMS loudness metering.

10 FX slots per insert

Stack built-in effects or CLAP plugins on every insert: 7-band EQ with spectrum view, compressor/limiter with sidechain input, tempo delay, reverb, modulation FX, saturator and mid/side shaper.

Saving and exporting

Save sessions as .thdr files or export a self-contained zipped loop package (.zip) with all samples included. Export mixes to WAV (16/24-bit dithered or 32-bit float), FLAC, OGG, MP3, individual mixer stems or Type-1 MIDI.

No sound? Check your system output device and volume first. On Linux, make sure an output device is selected in your audio settings. If you built Thunder from source on Linux, ensure ALSA development headers were installed before compiling.
