Common questions

Which systems does Thunder run on?

Windows (MSI installer), macOS (universal Intel and Apple Silicon disk image) and Linux (AppImage, archive and an old-Linux build for Ubuntu 20.04 era systems).

Why does Windows or Mac warn on first launch?

The binaries are not code-signed with Apple or Microsoft. On Mac, right-click Thunder and choose Open. On Windows, click More info in SmartScreen, then Run anyway.

Can I load my own plugins and samples?

Yes. Thunder scans and hosts third-party CLAP instrument and effect plugins, and loads WAV, MP3, OGG and FLAC audio files.

Does it need an account or internet connection?

No. Once installed, Thunder runs completely offline and saves projects directly to your disk.
